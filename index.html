Triage

About this demo

Triage is a working AI product management copilot, not a chatbot demo. It takes a messy, duplicate-filled backlog, clusters it, scores it with RICE and confidence, flags weak evidence, drafts stories for the top items, and leaves the final call to a human. Every item is scored and drafted in the open, so you can see not just what it shipped, but how it decided, and why.

A showcase of AI-native product work: discovery, prioritization, tradeoffs, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop decisions, across Healthcare, Fintech, Games, and E-commerce.

Review

About Review

Approve, edit, or reject each drafted story. Approving an item automatically creates a ClickUp task. Editing or rejecting never creates one. Every decision is recorded with its reason, and the item moves to Archived. Approved items export to ClickUp in the Triage Approved list.

Search and filters apply to drafted items in Review. Clear them to show all 25.

Archived

About Archived

Approved, edited, and rejected items leave the active inbox once decided. Approved items keep their ClickUp task link. Rejected items keep their reason. Nothing here is re-triaged, and re-importing an archived item does not bring it back. Approved items export to ClickUp in the Triage Approved list.
