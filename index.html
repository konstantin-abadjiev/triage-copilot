Case Study

Triage is a working AI product management copilot, not a chatbot demo. It takes a messy, duplicate-filled backlog, clusters it, scores it with RICE and confidence, flags weak evidence, drafts stories for the top items, and leaves the final call to a human. Every industry pack includes evals and a decision log, so you can see not just what it shipped, but how it decided, and why.

Problem: product teams get hundreds of requests in different words, with weak evidence and no clear order. Method: cluster duplicates, score with RICE and confidence, flag low evidence, draft only the top 25, and let a human decide. Result in the demo: 200 items triaged in about 1 hour 40 minutes, down from 11.5 hours by hand, with a cost of $3.84 per run, or $0.0192 per item, at Claude-like pricing.

Built Together: built by two collaborators to showcase how we use AI. One owns product logic, prompts, evals, and datasets. The other owns accounts and deployment, with keys only in back end environment variables.
