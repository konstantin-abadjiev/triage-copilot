Triage

About this demo

Triage is a working AI product management copilot, not a chatbot demo. It takes a messy, duplicate-filled backlog, clusters it, scores it with RICE and confidence, flags weak evidence, drafts a story for every scored item, and leaves the final call to a human. Every item is scored in the open, with its story written out, so you can see not just what it shipped, but how it decided, and why.

A showcase of AI-native product work: discovery, prioritization, tradeoffs, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop decisions, across Healthcare, Fintech, Games, and E-commerce.

About Backlog

Run triage stack-ranks the backlog by RICE (Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort), highest first. Approve or reject any item, in order. You can edit a story first, it stays in the backlog until you approve or reject it. Approving creates a ClickUp task automatically. Decided items move to Archived. Approved items export to ClickUp in the Triage Approved list.

About Archived

Approved and rejected items leave the active inbox once decided. Approved items keep their ClickUp task link. Rejected items keep their reason. Nothing here is re-triaged, and re-importing an archived item does not bring it back. Approved items export to ClickUp in the Triage Approved list.
